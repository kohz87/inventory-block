# Ersveil big beats (draft for the USER's approval, 31 Oct)

Ersveil 2.2.0 timeline. The answers it is built on are in V2_LOG (31 Oct, K discussion).

## How the beats work
- **Hidden until they fire.** Nothing in the lorebook spoils them. A beat reaches the narrator only when it fires, with
  a rumour cue a few days before where it makes sense.
- **The world moves; the player can step in.** Each beat fires on its date whether or not the player is there:
  - News travels outward with delays (Story Director's "N days after").
  - Being at the place when it fires gives the full scene and a hook (a Guild contract, a call for help).
  - Lasting changes (a city lost, a ruler dead, an order destroyed) go into Story Director's chronicle, so later
    replies and events know about them.
- **Four threads**, woven together:
  - **Floods → Tide:** the northern wildzone floods, which turn out to be the Tide's leading edge.
  - **Seals:** the Auvoth Depths stir. This is separate from the Tide and held by elite adventurers.
  - **Politics and war.**
  - **The underworld.**
- **Endgame if nobody intervenes:** the Tide is held at great cost. Auvoth is lost, the Frostbound and White-Horn
  lands are lost, and the Wardens of the Last Wall fall with their heroes.

## Year 1: CY 611, "The Turning came early"
| When | Beat | Thread |
|---|---|---|
| Haywane 12 | **The early flood.** Low-grade wildzone monsters (EG2–3) spill south onto the Bluewatch road and the Border marches. The signal horn sounds, and the Guild posts flood contracts. Everyone says the Turning came early. | Floods |
| Haywane 12–20 | **The Depths stir.** The same week, a surge from the upper bands hits the gates under the Seventh Seal Terrace. Elite parties and the Sealwardens hold it, and the Guild keeps it quiet. | Seals |
| Emberwane | **Who pays for the frontier.** The Compact Council quarrels over the flood's cost; Highmark offers troops on the Auvoth road, and the Guild refuses. | Politics |
| Frostfall 21 | **A harsher Turning** (the 611 Turning, stronger than usual). EG4–5 herds and a mammoth stampede reach the road, and a Border Kingdoms village burns. Still "just a bad Turning". | Floods |
| Yearsend | **Debts come due.** Shackles' Grey Ledger and Nightingale buy up war-supply contracts and frontier debts. | Underworld |

## Years 4–5: CY 614–615, "The Last Wall"
| When | Beat | Thread |
|---|---|---|
| CY 614, spring | **A new emperor** wins Highmark's war, and the Empire turns north at last. | Politics |
| CY 614, Turning | **Auvoth is lost** (lasting change). The Tide reaches the Guild's city: it is evacuated, then overrun. The Guild governs in exile, and the Depths are left behind the walls. | Tide |
| CY 615 | **The Last Wall.** The Wardens of the Last Wall hold the mountain roads, and the order falls with its named heroes (lasting change). The Tide is held there. | Tide |

## Year 6 onward: CY 616+, "After the Wall"
A new frontier line, refugee realms, a Guild without its city, and the Depths behind the Tide. These later beats stay
open for play.
