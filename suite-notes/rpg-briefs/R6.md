1. **Home card (section 7).** When the RPG feature is on and the story has no player character ("You"), Home shows a
   "Your character" card at the top: the text from the mockup, **Create your character** (opens the New character
   wizard) and **Not now** (hides the card for this chat; stored per chat; the Party tab's own "Create your character"
   stays). Gone as soon as a You character exists, or the RPG is off. Use Story Block's own Home card mechanism.
2. **Persona description (section 7).** The wizard's Who step fills Who they are from the active persona's description
   (first 240 characters, never splitting a character), as it already fills the name, with the note "From your
   persona's description (the first 240 characters). Change it freely: this is what the AI reads." Only for You, only
   when the field is untouched; a companion starts empty. Read the persona through SillyTavern's context the same way
   the name is read; never read other personas or settings.
3. **Hold the first send (section 7b).** RPG on, no You character, the reader presses Send (a normal send; not a
   swipe, regenerate, continue, impersonate, quiet or dry run): the message is held, never lost, and the dialog
   opens: "Make your character first", the text, the held message quoted, **Send without the RPG for now** (sends as
   usual, no dice this reply) and **Create your character, then send** (opens the wizard; the held message is sent
   when Create succeeds; cancelling the wizard puts the text back in the message box). Closing the dialog puts the text
   back. It asks once per chat: after "without", it never asks again in that chat (the Home card still reminds).
   Phone: a bottom sheet. Off means byte-identical behaviour: with the RPG off nothing is held.
4. **Companions who join (section 8).** Settings → RPG gets **Companions who join: Ask (default) / Automatically /
   Off**. With Ask or Automatically, the narrator is told one line form: `+ party <Name> | about: <text ≤240> | class:
   <I–IX> | best: <stat>` and `- party <Name>`.
   - `+ party`: makes a Character block (role character, companion) and an empty Skills block for that name: stats 10,
     the named best stat 14, Soul Class as written but never above the highest Soul Class in the party, bars full.
     Refused plainly (one Problems line, nothing made) when the name is taken, the party is full (8), or there is no
     room in the story's actions.
   - Ask: nothing is made by the line; the reply gets a card "<Name> joins the party: Add to party · Edit first · No".
     Add makes it as above; Edit first opens the wizard prefilled (name, about, class, best stat raised); No dismisses.
   - Automatically: made at once, listed in "Changed this reply"; a swipe/delete/Re-read takes it back (it is part of
     the reply's story, replay-safe like every other line).
   - `- party <Name>`: marks that companion as Left (kept, greyed, "Left" on the Party card), never deletes; a later
     `+ party` of the same name brings them back.
   - Off: no party lines in the grammar; byte-identical prompts.
