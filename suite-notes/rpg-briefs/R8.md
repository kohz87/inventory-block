**B — a skill can count when you defend (always on, RPG on).**
- The planner's foe line takes an optional `skill:`: `foe <foe> | target: <who> | defend: <stat> | skill: <skill>`.
  Its fixed instructions say to name a skill only when one of the defender's skills plausibly fits defending (a parry,
  a dodge, a shield, a counter-draw); otherwise none.
- prepare(): a foe line with a skill resolves it like an act's skill (skillFor): the skill's own stat wins over
  `defend`; its rank adds; an Empty resource rolls without it (with the same plain refusal note); a skill the defender
  lacks rolls untrained with the named stat. Hidden-field rules (review 160/163/164/166: statHidden, plain refusals)
  apply unchanged.
- The cue and the battle log name the defending skill (e.g. "Rat attacks Astra (Iaijutsu): BLOCKED …").
- Replay: a payload/receipt made before this (no `skill` on foe lines) rolls and tells exactly as before; recorded
  rules unchanged; add the skill only to new plans. Byte-identical with the RPG off.

**A — "Stat adds: full above 10" (a choice).**
- Settings → RPG → Stat adds gets a third option, "full above 10" (the stat − 5: 10 → +5, 14 → +9, 20 → +15, 8 → +3),
  beside "half the stat" and "a third of the stat". Presets (Gentle/Standard/Harsh) unchanged (half). Custom only.
- Rolls record the rule they used (as now), so old rolls replay with their own numbers; an old recorded rule set
  without the new value reads as before. The live odds bar and its comparison line use the chosen option.
- Checks and attacks and defence all use it (it is the stat bonus).
