## What to build
- **State** (a small domain or the scene domain, your call; say which): up to **6 rows**, each
  `{ name ≤40, kind ≤20, count 1–8 (default 1), pace km/day 1–200, condition ≤60, where ≤80 }`. Names match like
  other story names (case/space-insensitive). Older chats read as none.
- **Feature** `story-block.mounts`, label "Mounts", on by default. Off = no grammar line, no prompt line, no capture,
  nothing shown, prompts byte-identical to without this work.
- **Capture kind `mount`:** `+ mount Grey Mare | kind: horse | count: 1 | pace: 35 | where: ridden by Wessel`,
  `~ mount Burr | condition: lame | where: stabled at the Stray Oar`, `- mount Burr`. A missing pace takes the kind's
  default: horse 35, pony 30, camel 30, mule 25, donkey 20, ox 15, cart 18, wagon 18, else 30. A 7th row is refused
  with a plain Problem. One grammar line.
- **Narrator line** (per reply, only while there is a mount): e.g. "Mounts: Grey Mare (horse, 35 km/day, ridden by
  Wessel); Guild remounts ×4 (horse, 35 km/day); Burr (mule, tired, led)".
- **Queries (fixed interface; Story Director's R11 reads them):**
  - `mounts.list` → `[{ name, kind, count, pace, condition, where }]` (copies, story order).
  - `mounts.pace` → `{ pace, mounts, riders, short, slowest }` or `null` with no mount. `pace` = the slowest row's
    pace; `mounts` = total count; `riders` = the RPG party's members not Left when the RPG is on, else 1; `short` =
    max(0, riders − mounts); when `short > 0`, `pace` = 20 (they walk). `slowest` = that row's name.
- **Home card "Mounts"** (beside Save points, as in the mockup): one row per entry (icon, name with pencil, "kind ·
  condition · where", a pace pill), a note "The party travels at the slowest: N km/day riding." or, when short, "Not
  enough mounts for everyone: the party walks (20 km/day)." The card shows only while there is at least one mount.
  The pencil edits name/kind/count/pace/condition/where (an action on the latest reply, like other pencils).
- **Replay:** swipe, Re-read, delete take changes back; rebuild/reload equal; fuzz clean.
- Docs: docs/v2 GUIDE + SPEC (the domain, kind, queries, feature).
