## Lead decisions
- B1: hidden means hidden everywhere. `readSheet` honours `prompt: false`: a hidden name/field never reaches the
  planner prompt, the plan, the `[Rolls]` cue or the battle-log text sent to the narrator; a foe whose name is hidden
  is referred to by its slot ("Foe 2"), a hidden stat/skill is not offered.
- B2: the settle reserve is what a settle can really cost: one root each, and visits estimated from the block's own
  reply-end functions (bounded), not a flat 2048 per subject. Eight stock Characters must leave the story its normal
  room (test 8 and more).
- B3: the death card of a role `character` block always shows: role-character end cards sort before any other end
  card. (Other blocks' cards may still be cut; that is fine.)
- B4: a payload without `dice` (rolled before round 4) is re-read with the old cue-fit rule as well as the old dice,
  so its rounds and stop never change. Build the cross-version test from the review's steps.
- B5: shortened names stay distinct: when shortening, keep each name unique (e.g. keep a distinguishing tail, or fall
  back to the slot label with a one-line legend), never two participants with the same shown name.
- B6: role blocks are chat-scoped only: the checker refuses `role` on a `scope: 'character'` definition (plain
  message); views and the planner then agree by construction. Templates are already chat-scoped.
- B7: the used plan is cleared in `afterReceive` whether or not the feature is still on.
- B8: Settings → RPG combat saves are serialised: a save waits for the one in flight, and Reset compares against the
  pending value, so the last action always wins.
