## What to build
- **Feature** `story-director.trip`, label "Trips", on by default; off = nothing new in prompts, state or screens.
- **Capture kind `trip`:** `~ trip Wolfmere Outpost | by: riding | via: Hollin's Reach` (by: riding|foot|wagon|
  boat|ship|…; via optional, several comma-separated); `~ trip stop` (ends it where {{user}} is);
  `~ trip delay: 1 day | because: lame horse` (pushes every remaining arrival). One grammar line.
- **Planning (at capture, recorded so replay is exact):** from {{user}}'s current place to the destination along
  **known routes only** (the atlas: pack routes + `+ route` lines), through each `via`, shortest by km; legs
  `[{ from, to, km, kind, days, arrive }]` using travel.js's paces and per-leg round-up (`legDays`, `hopMode` for water
  legs). Pace for `by: riding`: Story Block's `mounts.pace` when that query answers (its `pace`; when `short > 0` it is
  already the walking pace), else the default ride pace; foot = default foot pace. `setOut` = the story time of the
  reply. No route → refused with a plain Problem naming the gap. A new `~ trip` mid-leg must continue along the
  current leg (onward to its end or back to its start) — "no turnings mid-leg"; otherwise refused plainly.
- **Position is derived, not stored:** from the story time vs the legs' times: current leg, km along it, arrived or
  not. At the last leg's arrival the trip shows "Arrived at X, <date>" until the place changes or a new trip starts.
- **Narrator tail line** while a trip is on: "On the road: Bluewatch Village → Hollin's Reach, 8 of 100 km, arrive
  Goldwane 16; then Wolfmere Outpost, Goldwane 19." When a leg's arrival has passed: "{{user}} has reached Hollin's
  Reach (Goldwane 16): write ~ place Hollin's Reach." (Story Director never moves Story Block's place itself.)
- **Home:** a "TRAVEL …" line under the header (as in the mockup) if Story Block's Home header lets a module add a
  line; otherwise a compact "On the road" card on Home. Say which in the report. Hidden with no trip.
- **Status card:** a **Travel** row (icon fa-route) after Coordinates, showing that reply's trip; left out with none.
- **Map** (atlas screen): the trip's route drawn as a thick accent line over the known routes; a "You" marker at the
  derived point along the current leg; an **On the road** panel (destination, mode and pace with the mount that sets it,
  set-out date, each leg with km, days, arrival, a progress bar on the current leg); and **movers you know of**:
  threads (agendas, events) with `leg:`/`arrive:` that world/known.js lets {{user}} know, as diamonds on their leg with
  "arrive <date>", listed in the panel. Hidden agendas, timeline events and secrets never appear (known.js).
  Phone: the panel goes under the map.
- **Replay:** swipe, Re-read, delete take changes back; rebuild/reload equal; the full suite green.
- Docs: README/docs for trips, the kind, the map panel.
