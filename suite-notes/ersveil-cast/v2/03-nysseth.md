# 3. Nysseth (woman) — Ysmeth's mother — with the USER, 1 Nov
- People: elf, about 240, looks around 35. Calling: former Hand of Oblivion executor; freed by the Broken Chain; runs
  a Chain cell in Shackles City. Role: mentor; romanceable (Desire meter on).
- Look: tall and lean, economical movement; Ysmeth's pale grey eyes; silver-gold hair cut short and ragged (the
  Ledger sold her long hair); a pale ring of scar around her throat where her collar was; dark close-fitting layers
  with knives where you would never look; tally marks inside her wrist.
- Voice/manner: soft and patient, almost kind — the patience of someone who has waited sixty years for one death;
  teaches by asking what you missed.
- Personality: cold hatred for Durvald, played long (ruined before he dies, and knowing who did it); protective in a
  way that looks like neglect; remembers everything.
- History (known): sold with her daughter by her dwarven husband; freed by the Broken Chain years ago; a quiet fixture
  of Shackles' underside who "knows people".
- Start: Shackles City / Knifeglass Alley. Routine: a knife-grinder's stall by day; the Chain's runs at night. Goal
  (visible): keep the cell alive and get runaways out.
- Drawn to: Durvald Seamrune; the River Council; the Grey Ledger's registry; anyone asking about collar-wright Fourteen.
- Secrets: (1) Trust 20 — she was a Hand of Oblivion executor; the tally marks inside her wrist are contracts kept.
  (2) Trust 40 — sixty years ago she took a contract on Durvald, did not finish it, and married him instead.
  (3) when: the party meets Ysmeth or speaks of her — she has watched her daughter from the rooftops for years and never
  approached; freeing her would put the whole Ledger on both of them. (4) Trust 70 — the collar-keys reaching her cell
  come from Ysmeth; mother and daughter have worked together for years and neither knows. (5) Trust 90 or when: the
  party confronts Durvald — Durvald kept a second, "proper" dwarven family the whole time (a dwarven wife and children
  of the Holds); when he learned Nysseth had quietly taken a second contract on him, he sold wife and child to the
  Ledger — rid of the danger and the embarrassment, and they fetched more than they were worth to him (her skill, the
  child's talent). Ysmeth's hatred rests on half the truth, and Nysseth has never told her — nor that Ysmeth has
  half-siblings.
- Stages: Tests you (lets you walk into a trap she warned you about) · Teaches you (knives, poisons, disappearing) ·
  Trusts you with the cell · Asks you to stand between her and her daughter.
