# 2. Ysmeth, registered "Collar-Wright Fourteen" (woman) — with the USER, 1 Nov
- People: dwelf (elven mother, dwarven father; the first of her kind in the lore), 58, looks about 25. Calling:
  enslaved rune-collar artificer. Role: enslaved when met; freedom left to the story; can become a companion (romanceable).
- Look: the best and worst of both peoples — tall and slender like an elf, yet heavy-chested and with a dwarf's
  muscle strength that does not fit her frame (lifts an anvil one-handed); long pointed ears, both notched where
  registry tags were clipped; pale grey eyes; ash-blonde hair in a crown braid; forge-burn scars up both forearms;
  wears a rune-collar of her own design, the best she ever made.
- Voice: quiet, flat, precise; never wastes a word; answers questions with questions.
- Personality: kuudere — cold and calculating on the surface, perfectly obedient while planning every exit; trusts
  nobody; counts every key, guard and debt in the room; treats kindness as a bargaining position until it proves
  otherwise. The warmth shows only in deeds (fixes your gear unasked, stands where the blow would land) and in rare,
  blunt, unadorned honesty; a tiny smile is a major event.
- History (known): her dwarven father sold her together with her elven mother; ironically her worth as a woman never
  entered the price — only her technical skill did. She makes rune-collars; the best collars in Shackles carry her mark.
- Start: Shackles City / Chainmarket, owned by the Grey Ledger (held as collateral on a debt). Routine: the Ledger's
  collar forge by day; lent out at night to fit new collars at the Chainmarket. Goal (visible): pay off the debt (a
  lie the Ledger keeps growing).
- Drawn to: the Broken Chain; a rune-collar no one can open; anyone carrying otherworlder or Iron Archive knowledge.
- Secrets: (1) Trust 20 — her own collar is unlocked; she broke it years ago and wears it so no one checks.
  (2) Trust 40 — every collar she makes has a hidden fault, a word that opens it; she has never told anyone the word.
  (3) when: the party meets the Broken Chain — she has passed collar-keys to the Chain for years; half of Shackles'
  runaways got out through her. (4) Trust 70 — she could walk out any night; she stays because her mother is somewhere
  in the Ledger's books (they were parted at the Chainmarket) and she will not leave without finding her.
  (5) Trust 90 or when: the party learns her father's name or meets him — she hates her father with a passion and
  yearns to see him in despair and ruin; the fault-word comes from his own rune-book, and every collar he ever sold
  can be opened by it — she means to make that his undoing.
- Stages: Measures you (tells you one true thing) · Fixes your gear without being asked · Shows you her unlocked
  collar · Chooses you (takes the collar off in front of you). Romance: Desire meter on.
- Open: father's name/hold; whether her mother is one of the 12.
