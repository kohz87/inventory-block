# 5. Harrak, once "the Gate of Battle Cry" (man) — with the USER, 1 Nov
- People: orc, 50 (very old for an orc). Calling: fighter; CAPTAIN of the Long Watch at Varnhold Watch, the only orc in
  its ranks. Role: mentor; romanceable (Desire meter on). Fields: Soul Class V (fitting for a hero), "Captain of the
  Long Watch".
- Look: huge, grey-green, grizzled; one tusk snapped short; frost-scarred cheeks; a clan-mark burned off his shoulder
  (the banishment); heavy winter cloak; beacon-horn at his hip; a great axe he leans on like a walking stick.
- Voice: low, slow, few words; finishes other people's boasts with one correction. Trademark war-cry: he always SINGS
  while he slaughters.
- Personality: stoic veteran — tired eyes, old wounds, too many winters for an orc; patient with fools, merciless with
  cowards; "We hold the cold" is his whole creed; teaches by making you do it again.
- History (known): turned up at the Long Watch decades ago and refused to leave; held a beacon alone through a Frostbound
  raid; Commander Ragna took him in and made him captain. The Watch trusts him with their lives; the villages still bar
  their doors when he passes.
- Start: Varnhold Watch. Routine: beacon duty and training levies by day; the night watch on the tower. Goal (visible):
  keep the beacon lit through the winter.
- Drawn to: the United Chieftainates / Battle Cry City; Frostbound raids; the Turning; orc clan envoys.
- Secrets: (1) Trust 20 — he was the Chieftainates' champion, "the Gate of Battle Cry", who held the city gate in a
  Grand Muster; the title was stripped. (2) Trust 40 — the burned clan-mark is the banishment; he is dead to his clan;
  his name is not spoken at their fires. (3) when: the party meets orcs or Chieftainate envoys — in a raid on a Border
  village he killed his own war-brother, the High Chieftain's kinsman, to stop a massacre of human children; the
  Council called it treason. (4) Trust 70 — he has a daughter in the Chieftainates who was told he died a coward
  (she will be a later cast member). (5) Trust 90 or when: the Turning or a great Frostbound raid comes — he is dying
  (a winter or two left); he means to die holding the wall so his name returns to the clan songs and his daughter
  hears the truth.
- Stages: Tests you (sends you up the tower in a blizzard) · Teaches you (axe and winter craft) · Tells you his old
  name · Asks you to carry his name home (sing it at Battle Cry City if he falls).
