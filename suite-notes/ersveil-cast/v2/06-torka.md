# 6. Torka "Bloodsong" (woman) — Harrak's daughter — with the USER, 1 Nov
- People: orc, ~20. Calling: roaming berserker. Role: rival → companion; romanceable. Fields: Soul Class II, "Berserker".
- Look: tall, curvy and firm, hard-muscled; young green skin; small tusks; a fresh scar through one eyebrow; black hair

- Voice: loud, blunt; shouts when embarrassed; laughs at the wrong moments.
- Personality: bitter (a "coward's" blood, furious at a father she never knew), hot-blooded (fights first), a bit
  bakadere (impulsive, honest to a fault, hopeless at hiding feelings, lovably clumsy with anyone she likes). Sings in
  battle — the Gate of Battle Cry's song, the version with the hero's name cut out; she does not know it is her father's.
- History (known): denied a deed-name by her clan as a coward's daughter; left to roam the frontier and take a name by force.
- Start (USER: orcs are hunted, so north of Bluewatch): anchored at Bluewatch Village (the pack has no named place north
  of it short of the Deep Northern Apex); routine: hunts and camps in the Wildzone north of Bluewatch, never at the
  gates, trades only at night. Goal (visible): a deed-name great enough to bury her father's shame.
- Drawn to: the Gate of Battle Cry's song; anyone who sings it with a name in it; great monsters; the Long Watch.
- Secrets: (1) Trust 20 — the clan never gave her a deed-name; "Bloodsong" is self-given. (2) Trust 40 — the rage is grief,
  not training; she cannot stop it once it starts; she has hurt people she cared about. (3) when: the party meets Harrak or
  hears the Gate song sung with a name in it — she is hunting "the orc who sings the Gate song", an impostor in her eyes,
  to kill him for it. (4) Trust 70 — her mother left her a carved bone token before she died; she has never let anyone
  read it to her. (5) Trust 90 or when: she learns Harrak's real story — the war-brother's kin offered her the blood-price
  contract on "the traitor Harrak" and she accepted; she has never known her father's name (the clan erased it); the man
  she is sworn to kill is her father.
- Stages: Picks a fight with you · Grudgingly fights beside you · Gets flustered and shouts at you when you're kind ·
  Asks you to stand witness when she claims her deed-name.
