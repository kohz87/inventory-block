# 10. "Hush" — Moonrain-on-the-Roofs (woman) — with the USER, 1 Nov
- People: spiritfolk, formed ~300 years ago from moonlit rain. Calling: Vargate's night lamplighter and guide of the
  lost. Role: mentor; romanceable. Fields: Soul Class III, "Lamplighter of Vargate".
- Look (USER): ethereal, but with a girl-next-door face and a regular figure; a semi-physical form of silver rain,
  solid enough to hold a hand, translucent at the edges; hair that falls like rain that never lands; droplets drifting
  upward off her shoulders; a faint silver-blue glow at night; a hooded grey coat that always looks damp; a long brass
  lamplighter's pole; a plain oil lantern (never a bound one).
- Voice: soft, unhurried, like rain on a roof; people fall asleep listening to her.
- Personality: warm, gentle, patient, quietly funny; notices who is lost before they know it; mothers runaways, drunks
  and frightened newcomers; cannot stay in a room lit by a bound spirit (goes pale and leaves).
- History (known): has lit Vargate's lamps longer than anyone remembers; ward-lords tolerate her because their doors are
  safer when she walks; children call her "the rain lady".
- Start: Vargate. Routine: sleeps by day; lights the lamps at dusk; walks the wards all night guiding the lost. Goal
  (visible): keep Vargate's night a little kinder.
- Drawn to: bound-spirit lamps and binders; lost people; the Ashen Veil's temple ward (avoids it); the Domain of Oren;
  Crown Keep's lamps.
- Lothar tie (USER): the child who broke her lamp in a Highmark manor a century ago was Lothar's grandmother; the great
  bound lamps of Crown Keep — on Lothar's night watch — hold her kin.
- Secrets: (1) Trust 20 — she is the "lamp-thief" of Vargate's ghost stories; the rich wards' bound lamps go dark because
  she frees them one at a time. (2) Trust 40 — a freed lamp-spirit is too small to live alone; she carries them inside her
  form until they are strong; that is why she glows brighter every year. (3) when: the party meets a binder or sees a
  bound-spirit lamp — she was bound herself for a hundred years in a lamp in a Highmark manor, until a child broke it to
  see the pretty light; she has looked for that child's family ever since, to repay them — the child was Lothar's
  grandmother. (4) Trust 70 — carrying so many small spirits is slowly unmaking her; one day she will simply end, like
  rain stopping, and she has made peace with it. (5) Trust 90 or when: the party reaches Crown Keep — the great bound
  lamps lighting Crown Keep hold spirits of her own kin from Oren; she means to free them one day, even if it darkens
  the imperial palace.
- Stages: Walks you home · Teaches you the doors of Vargate · Lets you see her free a spirit · Asks you to remember
  her (rain stops).
- USER (1 Nov): take all seven.
  * Limit: cannot stay near bound spirits; Highmark's palaces are full of bound lamps — Crown Keep would be agony; to
    meet Lothar's family or free her kin she must walk into the place that hurts her most.
  * In play: every door and safe route in Vargate; senses bound spirits and spirit-powered traps; her voice can calm and
    put people to sleep; small spirit pacts; her glow lights the dark. No fighter.
  * Tells: droplets drift faster when she is moved; her glow dims when she is sad; touches every lamp she passes, gently.
  * Threats: ward-lords who keep losing expensive lamps; binders — a spirit carrying dozens of small spirits is a fortune.
  * Gentle moral edge: freeing a lamp sometimes takes the only light a poor family has; she chooses anyway, leaves them
    candles, and it bothers her.
  * Romance mark (top stage): she makes it rain softly in your cupped hands, just for you — to spiritfolk, giving part
    of yourself.
  * Cast fun: Nerissa's racy shanties make her blush into drizzle; sea and rain, almost sisters.
