# 4. Rika (woman) — with the USER, 1 Nov
- People: human (River Confederacy barge-girl), 22. Calling: self-taught mage; power ridiculously strong but
  unrefined. Cannot read (never schooled). Role: hidden agenda; can become a companion (romanceable). Fields: Soul
  Class IV, "Self-taught (no spell grammar)".
- Look: petite but outrageously curvy figure; hair turned pure white when she awakened, worn long, falling over most of her face (she hides behind it);
  sharp green eyes; freckles; spell-burn scars on both hands hidden in fingerless gloves; a Startrace master's
  travelling coat far too big for her, the Tower insignia torn off.
- Magic: overwhelming raw force, no control — overkill, collateral, burns herself; learns by watching and instinct,
  never from books.
- Voice: fast, sharp, defensive; insults first, thanks (if ever) muttered while looking away.
- Personality: tsundere — proud, prickly, denies caring loudly; flusters badly when caught being kind; picks fights
  with anyone Tower-trained; hides that she cannot read (bluffs, memorises, asks others to "check" things);
  underneath, guilt and terror of what her power cost.
- History (known): a barge-girl from the Confederacy, never schooled; turned up in Auvoth last winter in a stolen coat;
  throws spells that make licensed mages stare.
- Start: Auvoth / Pale Market. Routine: unlicensed spellwork for cheap crews by day; at night she circles the Seventh
  Seal Court's records, which she cannot read. Goal (visible): a Guild licence and a crew.
- Drawn to: Startrace masters; Master Albrecht Somner; the Hollow Aurora Zone; Veyr's seals; anyone who can read old script.
- Secrets: (1) Trust 20 — the coat is real; she took it off a dead Startrace master. (2) Trust 40 — she was a hired
  porter on Somner's CY 610 expedition north, the only one who came back. (3) when: the party meets a Startrace master
  or hears Somner's name — the expedition brought forty condemned prisoners bought from Highmark to feed a leyline tap
  with their deaths; she kept the tally (by knots — she cannot write). (4) Trust 70 — her magic woke there: when the
  forty died at once she took in far more of their release than any commoner should; her power is made of them, and
  she still feels the hunger. (5) Trust 90 or when: the party heads for the Hollow Aurora Zone — she is trying to atone
  by stopping the Tide: the tap is still open and growing, feeding the next Abyssal Tide (and Somner may still be
  there, feeding it); she came to Auvoth for Veyr's sealing records to close it herself — and needs a crew, and
  someone who can read them, which is why she courts the party with half-truths.
- Stages: Tolerates you ("It's not like I wanted your help") · Shows you a spell no one taught her · Stops lying about
  the coat (and admits she can't read) · Asks you to go north with her, and admits she is afraid.
- USER (1 Nov): take all six.
  * Threat: the Church Inquest hunts anyone who absorbs the release too well; Startrace's Archmaster denies the
    northern records — both want her silenced or caught if they learn. Drawn to (added): Church Inquest agents.
  * Power in play: no small magic (lighting a candle sets the table on fire); every big spell leaves new burns; in a
    slaughter she grows stronger (she absorbs the dying) — a temptation the narrator shows in fights.
  * Tells: tugs her hair further over her face when lying or flustered; pretends to read by tracing letter shapes;
    carries the expedition's knotted tally cord and worries at it.
  * Romance mark (top stage): asks you to teach her to read; the first word she ever writes is your name.
  * Moral edge: to stop the Tide, would she use deaths to power herself? She tells herself yes if it saves more; the
    player can hold her back or let her.
  * Tie to Temiwa — new secret (Trust 50, or when: the party meets Temiwa): coming back half-frozen from the north
    she passed through Bluewatch; Temiwa treated her, sensed the abnormal absorption, and never reported her. Each owes
    the other; neither has said a word.
