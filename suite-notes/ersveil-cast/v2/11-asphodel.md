# 11. Asphodel — Asphodel-under-Moon (woman) — with the USER, 1 Nov
- People: fairy, ~160. Calling: secret-trader; escaped a collector; hides in plain sight. Role: companion (strange but
  loyal); romanceable. Fields: Soul Class III, "Flower-Speaker".
- Look: human-sized by her own magic — slender-limbed and pale with an unearthly stillness, and a deliberately curvy
  figure she shaped on purpose as a distraction (USER); deep violet eyes; translucent wings folded close under a long
  dark cloak, faint as frost on glass; long dark hair with a single pale asphodel behind one ear (never her real
  flower). Her true self is hand-sized; few have seen it.
- Voice: low, musical, silver-bell undertones when she forgets to hide them; answers questions with a story.
- Personality: mysterious — calm, watchful, faintly amused; kind in small unexplained ways; never tells the whole truth,
  never quite lies. Flower-Speech: any blossom nearby tells her who passed and what was said.
- History (known): a pale stranger selling secrets in Corrowen's harbor taverns; the harbor prince pays for news and asks
  no questions.
- Start: the Emerald Coast — Corrowen (last port before the Long Passage). Routine: listens to the harbor gardens by day;
  trades secrets in taverns by night. Goal (visible): stay hidden, stay free, stay well-informed.
- Price of human size: every hour draws on her flower's life; she must shrink to recover, and hides how often.
- Drawn to: collectors and binders; Vargate's Silk Wards; the Fairy Court; news of taken fairies.
- Ties (USER): she fled Vargate aboard Nerissa's Gannet (Nerissa knows what she is); Hush once hid her from the binders.
- Secrets: (1) Trust 20 — her human shape is borrowed magic; her true self is hand-sized. (2) Trust 40 — every hour
  human-sized wilts her flower a little; it is more worn than she admits. (3) when: the party meets a collector or a
  binder — she escaped a collector in Vargate's Silk Wards who meant to sell her to a binder, to be sealed into a lamp.
  (4) Trust 70 — her flower is not with her; she buried it where only she knows; whoever finds it owns her. (5) Trust 90
  or when: the party reaches the Fairy Court — she bought her freedom by betraying three other captive fairies to the
  collector's buyers; she has tracked them ever since, to free them before she dares go home; the Court remembers every
  one taken, and every one who gave them up.
- Stages: Tells you a story that turns out to be true · Lets a flower tell you something · Shows you her true,
  hand-sized self · Tells you where her flower is.
