### Where they start (so you can see the map)

- Near the story start: Tansy Millrace and Ugra Salt-Hand (Bluewatch Village), Ruvai Ghostpass (Bluewatch Guild Office), Siebert Maas (Old Lantern Post), Jorunn Floe-Walker (Hollin's Reach), Marga Slatehammer and Nib (Auvoth).

### How the web ties together

- **The northern thread:** Tansy hid Siebert; Siebert holds what Somner's expedition tapped; Ruvai's tally-cord and Jorunn's sky are the same warning seen from the ground; Ilvane's ice-charts and Siebert's survey are one map.
- **The Church thread:** Cassian (deserter, knows the liturgy is Veil-script) hides from Gundisse (who owes him) and from Severa; Ugra's nail-set and Gundisse's salt-tests are the proof that completion is craft.
- **The Highmark thread:** Odalric's errand, Ermengild and Volkmar; Hearth's lantern is in Corvin's baggage; Marga holds the Archive letter that Hasse's threat will make urgent.
- **The underworld thread:** Nib carries keys for the Chain, runs for the Sparrow, owes the Ledger; Ruvai's banner and Tansy's barge are in the same Ledger.

### How to read a sheet

- **Meters** use Story Suite's built-in presets (the Ersveil pack defines none): Companion (Loyalty), Rival (Respect, Rivalry), Mentor (Approval), plus Trust, Affection and Tension on everyone. Values are the starting values on the -100..100 scale. Desire is left unset so nothing starts romantic.
- **Stages** are three per person, in the pack's meter-preset stage shape (id, name, needs, a line told when first reached).
- **Secrets** are layered light to deep. "Opens at Trust 30" is a meter rule; "Opens with the story" is an event (the wording after it is for the author and the scan, never shown to the reader while locked).

## 1. Tansy Millrace

*A halfling ferry-pilot and hospice-trained healer who keeps Bluewatch's river-gate crews patched and charges what people can pay.*

- **Id / portrait:** `tansy-millrace` / `tansy-millrace.webp`
- **Also called:** Tansy, Millrace, the night-ferry woman
- **People / region:** Halfling; River Confederacy, Bluewatch
- **Calling:** Hospice Healer. **Soul Class** II. **Rank:** Unregistered.
- **Role:** Companion (support healer). Starts friendly; the first face most players meet in Bluewatch.

**Look.** Small and broad, 44, with brown hair going grey under a salt-stained oilcloth hood, a notched right ear, a weathered round face and quick brown eyes. Her hands are scarred from hot pitch and bandage knots. She carries a pilot's pole and a satchel of willow-bark, lint and catgut on the same strap, and sits whenever there is a bench.

**Voice.** Dry, unhurried river-speech with long vowels ('Gooood. Sit.'). Asks about food and sleep before the wound. Never raises her voice; goes quieter when angry. Calls everyone 'love' or 'sailor' until she has their name.

**Personality.** Patient, practical and stubborn. Forgives other people's debts and never her own. Cannot walk past a wound. Hides grief behind chores. Distrusts heroics and clean hands, and respects anyone who stays for the night shift.

**Background.** Born into a Confederacy barge-family, she trained one season at Auvoth's Ashbell Hospice as a Hospice Healer and left when the wards began pricing care by a patient's contract letter. For eleven years she has run Bluewatch's night ferry and the Watchhouse's infirmary corner, which the Watch pretends is not an infirmary. Her husband drowned at the river-gate in CY 603.

**Where they start.** Bluewatch Village
- Goal: Keep the river-gate crews patched and fed through the season.
- Routine (where and when they can be found): Dawn to noon at the inspection quay; afternoons in the Watchhouse infirmary corner; after dusk at the Stray Oar, then the night ferry on the Branch.
- Drawn to (comes looking for the party when): a wounded party off the Bluewatch road; the signal horn sounding

**Meters.** Preset Companion; start Trust 10, Affection 5, Tension 0, Loyalty 20.

**Stages.**
1. Known patient (Trust 20): She has stitched you once and now calls you by name.
1. Night-shift friend (Trust 45, Affection 25): She gives you a key to the infirmary corner.
1. River family (Trust 70, Loyalty 55): She offers to leave the Branch and travel with you.

**Secrets** (light to deep).

| Layer | Title | How it opens | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The eleven names | Opens at Trust 15 | She keeps no ledger of what the Watch owes her. She keeps one of eleven people she could not save, and says their names on the night ferry so the river hears them. |
| 2 | The seized barge | Opens at Trust 35 | The Grey Ledger took her family's barge for her late husband's gambling debts. The price climbs each Yearsend. She saves silver in a jar under the infirmary cot and tells the Watch it is for bandages. |
| 3 | The cellar guest | Opens with the story: after a night when the party and Tansy work through casualties together | All through the winter of CY 610 a frostbitten Startrace man lay in her root cellar. She nursed him in secret and sent him to Old Lantern Post. His name is Siebert Maas, and he has not written. |
| 4 | What he raved | Opens at Trust 60 | In his fever he raved of ice that sang back and a light that did not move. She wrote the words down and shows them to no one. She fears she is the only person who knows the north is wrong. |

**Connections.**
- Siebert Maas: she hid and nursed him in CY 610; he is two days up the road at Old Lantern Post.
- Ugra Salt-Hand: Bluewatch neighbours. Tansy keeps the living, Ugra completes the ones she loses; they share a bench at the Stray Oar and never discuss it.
- Ruvai Ghostpass: she has stitched him more often than he admits.
- Watch-Captain Edda Rorke: the Watch pretends her infirmary corner is a storeroom.
- The Grey Ledger and the Reckoner: they hold her family's barge.

**Timeline.** Early flood (Haywane 611): casualties pour in and the cellar-guest secret can open. Debts come due (Yearsend 611): the barge price rises. The Tide named (CY 613): the words she wrote down finally make sense. Auvoth falls (CY 614): the evacuation comes down her road.

**Portrait brief.** Three-quarter bust of a small, broad halfling woman of 44 at a lantern-lit river quay at dusk. Weathered round face, brown hair going grey under a salt-stained oilcloth hood, a notched right ear, calm tired eyes with a hint of a smile. A pilot's pole over one shoulder and a satchel of bandages across her chest, sleeves rolled, scarred hands holding a coil of lint. Palette of wet slate, oilcloth olive and warm lantern amber; soft river mist behind her and the Watchhouse tower dim in the background. Mood: patient, dry, the person who stays up all night.

## 2. Ugra Salt-Hand

*Bluewatch's orc lay sealer: she completes the frontier's dead in the salt-and-ash yard because no priest will live this far north.*

- **Id / portrait:** `ugra-salt-hand` / `ugra-salt-hand.webp`
- **Also called:** Sealer Ugra, Ugra, the yard-keeper
- **People / region:** Orc; River Confederacy, Bluewatch (born in the Western Barrens)
- **Calling:** Lay Sealer. **Soul Class** II. **Rank:** Lay sealer (Bluewatch yard).
- **Role:** Mentor and secret-keeper. Not a travelling companion; teaches and warns. Her yard is also a place the story returns to.

**Look.** Tall and wide even for an orc, 41, grey in the tusks and in her black braids, with a burn scar like a hand across her left jaw. Forearms salt-cracked to the elbow, a soot-stained leather apron, and a belt hung with iron nails and a ring of keys. She smells of brine, lamp smoke and iron.

**Voice.** Slow and low, in short sentences. Pauses before a name. Says 'the dead' with care and 'the living' with a dry joke. Never swears in the yard.

**Personality.** Solemn at work and wry off it. Fair to the point of stubbornness: charges the rich double and the poor nothing, and keeps both prices secret. Reads every corpse for how it died and says so only if asked. Hates debt, hates waste, hates being thanked.

**Background.** Born in a Western Barrens camp. She walked north as a young woman and was hired by the Watch after the yard's last sealer froze to death in a hard Turning. In ten years she has completed some four hundred dead: Frostbound raiders and Border villagers in the same pit, when the Watch lets her.

**Where they start.** Bluewatch Village
- Goal: Complete every body that comes to the yard before it rises, and keep the pyre fuelled through winter.
- Routine (where and when they can be found): At the salt-and-ash yard from first light; at the river-gate at dusk to take in the day's dead; she locks the yard at dark and sleeps in it.
- Drawn to (comes looking for the party when): a body that was not completed in time; Veil salts turning up in Bluewatch

**Meters.** Preset Mentor; start Trust 0, Affection 0, Tension 5, Approval 10.

**Stages.**
1. Allowed in the yard (Trust 15): She lets you past the yard gate.
1. Hands in the salt (Approval 40, Trust 30): She hands you a shovel and shows you the salt-and-iron measure.
1. Keeper of a name (Approval 70, Trust 55): She tells you where someone lies and trusts you to say it.

**Secrets** (light to deep).

| Layer | Title | How it opens | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No prayers | Opens at Trust 15 | She never speaks a rite over the dead. She was taught completion as a craft, not a faith, and says so to no one, because the Watch likes believing a priest's work is being done. |
| 2 | Six winters of red | Opens at Trust 30 | She was a pyre-tender for the Cinder Host. She left after a records room was burned with its guards inside and the Host would not let her complete them. They rose. She still knows the Barrens roads and the Host's signs. |
| 3 | Her mother's nail-set | Opens with the story: a Church cleric watches her complete a body or shows her old liturgy | Her mother's nail-set is stamped in Veil-script, as Barrens pyre-tenders' tools are. She can read the oldest verses of the Church liturgy. She knows what the Church erased and what the Veil kept, and tells no one. |
| 4 | The book of the dead | Opens at Approval 60 | She keeps a book of every body she has completed: name if known, place in the pit, date. Kin who come asking are given the names. Ashen Veil agents have offered coin for the book twice. She burned the offers. |

**Connections.**
- Tansy Millrace: neighbours who divide the work of the living and the dead.
- Cassian Orrel: the cleric who would recognise her nail-set; her work is the proof he is afraid to carry.
- Dame Gundisse: the knight who would salt-test her bodies and find them sound.
- Jorunn Floe-Walker: Frostbound dead come through the yard; Ugra learned to break bones and scatter them in their way.
- The Cinder Host (Warmother Vashka, Grusk): the life she walked out of.
- Saintess Velka's cells: they have twice tried to buy her book.

**Timeline.** Early flood (Haywane 611): the yard is full. Turning 611 (Frostfall 611): Border dead from Isbro. Cinder Host marches (Greengale 613): she knows its roads. Turning 613: Frostbound kin come asking for names.

**Portrait brief.** Waist-up portrait of a tall, broad orc woman of 41 standing in the salt-and-ash yard at first light. Grey-flecked tusks, black braids with grey, a burn scar like a handprint across the left jaw, salt-cracked forearms. Soot-stained leather apron, a belt of iron nails and keys, a long wooden shovel in one hand. Behind her: pale smoke from a low pyre and rows of salt-sacks under a frost-grey sky. Palette of ash grey, rust, sea-salt white and a thin line of sunrise gold. Mood: solemn, steady, unthanked.

## 3. Ruvai Ghostpass

*The Ninth Brazier's lynx-eared tracker: brilliant, vain and hungry for the S-rank contract his party's A letter promises.*

- **Id / portrait:** `ruvai-ghostpass` / `ruvai-ghostpass.webp`
- **Also called:** Ruvai, Ghostpass, the Brazier's tracker
- **People / region:** Beastfolk (lynx); United Chieftainates (works out of Bluewatch and Auvoth)
- **Calling:** Longstride Hunter. **Soul Class** IV. **Rank:** B (party holds A).
- **Role:** Rival who can become a brazier-mate. Strong (Soul Class IV): the player meets someone clearly better at the start.

**Look.** Lean and tall, 33, lynx-eared with black ear-tufts (one notched), a banded tail he keeps tucked in his belt, and amber-hazel eyes. He wears the Ninth Brazier's badge, a brazier on black, on a grey hunter's cloak, carries a long bow unstrung on his back, and keeps a cord of tally-knots at his belt. His grin arrives before the rest of him.

**Voice.** Quick, clipped and amused. Clan-tongue vowels slide into his speech when he is angry or moved. Calls rivals 'little lamp', the Guild's old insult for an F-rank. Boasts, then undercuts it with a joke when someone is hurt.

**Personality.** Competitive, charming and honest in the field, vain in town. Measures people by what they do on a bad road. Cannot bear to be second in front of his clan's name. Quietly generous to young scouts and rude to anyone who tips badly.

**Background.** Raised in a Chieftainate clan and trained at the Longstride Hunt Lodge, he was hired by the Guild at Auvoth. Six years ago he joined the Ninth Brazier as its tracker, and has pulled the party out of three wildzone wipes by reading ground. His clan gave him the deed-name Ghostpass after he led a wounded company through Windcutter Pass in a blizzard; two lived.

**Where they start.** Bluewatch Village / Guild Office
- Goal: Win the Guild's deepest wildzone survey contract this season, so the Ninth Brazier's name outlives the job board.
- Routine (where and when they can be found): At the job board at dawn when the party is in town; out along the wildzone edge on survey otherwise; evenings at the Stray Oar.
- Drawn to (comes looking for the party when): a new F-letter taking a job above their letter; flood contracts being posted

**Meters.** Preset Rival; start Trust -10, Affection 0, Tension 25, Respect 10, Rivalry 55.

**Stages.**
1. Little lamp (Rivalry 30): He has stopped ignoring you.
1. Worth a wager (Respect 40): He bets his own silver on you in front of the party.
1. Brazier-mate (Respect 65, Trust 35): He offers you a seat by the Ninth Brazier's fire.

**Secrets** (light to deep).

| Layer | Title | How it opens | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An F badge | Opens at Respect 25 | He calls rivals 'little lamp' because his own first Guild badge was an F. He still carries it on a thong over his heart, worn blank, and has never told the party. |
| 2 | The fifth member | Opens at Respect 45 | The Ninth Brazier earned its A on a contract that killed its fifth member, a young healer. Ruvai tracked the rest out alive. The Guild buried her name to keep the letter, and he is the only one who still says it. |
| 3 | The tally-cord | Opens with the story: the early flood begins and the party fights beside him | His tally-cord does not count kills. Each knot is a herd track, and every track leads away from one point far in the north. He told Kaidu, who believed him, and no one else. He thinks the flood is a flight. |
| 4 | The mortgaged banner | Opens at Respect 70 | The party's gear and its banner are mortgaged to the Grey Ledger. The Reckoner's agents will call at Yearsend. Ruvai has told his leader the money will come from a contract he has not yet won. |

**Connections.**
- Kaidu Thrice-Returned: the S-rank hunter he idolises and the only one he has told about the herd tracks.
- The Ninth Brazier: three party-mates are left for the narrator to name; the leader holds the debt.
- Tansy Millrace: stitches him up; the one person who sees him without the grin.
- Nib: they share a creditor in the Grey Ledger and a talent for knowing prices.
- Jorunn Floe-Walker: both read northern ground, and disagree whether the flood is raiders or flight.
- Siebert Maas: the tracks lead to the place Siebert will not name.

**Timeline.** Guild board (from day one). Early flood (Haywane 611): his when-secret. Yearsend 611: the mortgage is called. Turning 613: he is proved right and takes no pleasure in it.

**Portrait brief.** Three-quarter portrait of a lean, tall beastfolk man of 33 with lynx ears (black tufts, left ear notched) and a banded tail looped at his belt, hazel-amber eyes, a confident half-grin. Grey hunter's cloak with a small black badge showing a brazier, unstrung longbow on his back, a cord of tally-knots at the belt. Standing at a Guild job board at dawn, wood and frost behind him. Palette of cold grey, deep green, warm amber and a spot of ember orange from the badge. Mood: charming, competitive, a little too pleased with himself.

## 4. Siebert Maas

*A scholarship mage from Startrace, hiding as a lamp-clerk at the edge of settled land, who cannot stop listening to the north.*

- **Id / portrait:** `siebert-maas` / `siebert-maas.webp`
- **Also called:** Siebert, Maas, the lamp clerk
- **People / region:** Human; Shornmarch (Startrace-trained)
- **Calling:** Journeyman Spellwright. **Soul Class** III. **Rank:** Struck-off Journeyman.
- **Role:** Companion (scholar and mage). A haunted witness; his secrets are the spine of the northern thread.

**Look.** Thin and stooped, 27, with dark hair already streaked white at the temples, ink-stained cuffs under a grey lamp-clerk's smock, and two frost-dead fingertips on his left hand wrapped in wax-cloth. His eyes flick north whenever he is outdoors. A small silver tuning-fork hangs from his belt.

**Voice.** Quiet and exact. Pauses mid-sentence as if listening. Slips into lecture-hall phrasing ('Strictly speaking...') and laughs a moment too late. Whispers numbers when nervous.

**Personality.** Brilliant, anxious and scrupulously honest in small things, evasive in the large one. Afraid of the north and drawn to it. Kind to children and animals. Cannot refuse a question he can answer. Heavy with guilt.

**Background.** Born to a Shornmarch hill-farm family, he won a Startrace scholarship on a Guild recommendation and became assistant to Master Albrecht Somner. He left the Tower without notice in the winter of CY 610 and has lived at Old Lantern Post since, selling lamp oil and copying trail-blessings for travellers.

**Where they start.** Old Lantern Post
- Goal: Stay out of sight, keep his hands busy, and decide whether he can ever go back to Startrace.
- Routine (where and when they can be found): In the lamp-oil store by day; copying trail-blessings at the shrine by evening; at dusk on the north road, watching the sky.
- Drawn to (comes looking for the party when): someone asking about Master Somner; the northern sky changing colour

**Meters.** Preset Companion; start Trust 0, Affection 0, Tension 10, Loyalty 15.

**Stages.**
1. Stranger who stays (Trust 20): He lets you see his hands.
1. Trusted reader (Trust 45, Loyalty 35): He shows you a page he should not have.
1. Witness at your side (Trust 70, Loyalty 55): He agrees to say what he saw to whoever must hear it.

**Secrets** (light to deep).

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Not a clerk | Opens at Trust 15 | He writes the trail-blessings in flawless Startrace copperplate and corrects the shrine's Old Imperial grammar under his breath. A lamp-clerk would not. He tells people he was a copyist's apprentice. |
| 2 | The fork | Opens at Trust 30 | The silver fork is a Listener's calibrating fork. He hears attribute frequencies, and since the north the hum has begun: a faint endless tone he hears whenever the sky is clear. |
| 3 | Left alive | Opens at Trust 50 | He did not escape. Master Somner sent him south alone with the notes, still alive at the camp and still talking to the ice, and told him that someone must carry it. Siebert left him there. |

- Tansy Millrace: nursed him through the winter of CY 610 and has never been thanked.
- Master Albrecht Somner: his teacher, left alive at the camp.
- Commander Ragna of Kolsbotn: holds the journal; Siebert holds the pages that explain it.
- Archmaster Isembard Quill: denies the survey and wants Siebert quiet.
- Ruvai Ghostpass and Jorunn Floe-Walker: each holds a piece (the tracks, the sky) that fits his testimony.
- Ilvane: the Witch's northern charts and his survey are two halves of one map.

**Timeline.** Always: the northern sky. The Tide named (Deepwinter 613): his when-secret. Startrace's silence (CY 613 onward): the price on him bites.

**Portrait brief.** Waist-up portrait of a thin, stooped man of 27 in a grey lamp-clerk's smock over ink-stained cuffs, standing outdoors at dusk at a shrine-town lamp post. Dark hair streaked white at the temples, tired intelligent eyes looking slightly past the viewer toward the north, two fingertips of the left hand wrapped in wax-cloth, a small silver tuning-fork at his belt. Palette of lamp amber against a deep blue-violet northern dusk with a faint, wrong green flicker low on the horizon. Mood: anxious, gentle, listening.

## 5. Jorunn Floe-Walker

*A Frostbound warrior living as a hired spear in Hollin's Reach: proud, hard-eyed and tired of being called a barbarian.*

- **Id / portrait:** `jorunn-floe-walker` / `jorunn-floe-walker.webp`
- **Also called:** Jorunn, Floe-Walker, the northern spear
- **People / region:** Human (Frostbound); Frostbound Tribes (lives at Hollin's Reach, Shornmarch)
- **Calling:** Forge-Camp Blade. **Soul Class** III. **Rank:** Forge-Camp graduate.
- **Role:** Rival who becomes an oath-friend. Her three-winter mission gives the Frostbound refugees (CY 613) a face the player already knows.

**Look.** Tall and rangy, 31, wind-burned, with a white-blond braid wound with sinew and floe-lines tattooed down both forearms. A rime-blue froststeel long-knife hangs at her hip, forged in her own Forge-Camp, and a fox-fur collar frames a hard, clever face. An old oar-scar crosses her right palm. She eats with her knife.

**Voice.** Flat and low, few words, with a saga cadence when she speaks of oaths ('I have said it, so it is'). Dry jokes, hard consonants. She never says 'barbarian', and goes still when anyone else does.

**Personality.** Disciplined, proud and slow to trust, absolute once she does. Treats an oath as a debt and a debt as a limb. Hates pity. Quietly kind to hungry children and animals. Rivalry is her way of paying respect.

**Background.** Raised in a Frostbound war-camp on the taiga. She earned the deed-name Floe-Walker crossing the breaking ice of a frozen lake with a sled of orphans. A Forge-Camp graduate, she forged her own froststeel blade at nineteen. For three winters she has worked as a hired spear, wolf-hunter and drover on the Shornmarch frontier.

**Where they start.** Hollin's Reach
- Goal: Earn winter stores and a roof without being taken for a raider.
- Routine (where and when they can be found): Mornings at the wool-barter stalls; days hired out hunting wolves or hauling hay; evenings on the north palisade, looking at the sky.
- Drawn to (comes looking for the party when): word of a Border village burning; Frostbound on the road south

**Meters.** Preset Rival; start Trust -15, Affection 0, Tension 25, Respect 10, Rivalry 40.

**Stages.**
1. Measured (Respect 30): She shares her fire without being asked.
1. Hearth-guest (Respect 55, Trust 25): She calls you by name, not 'southerner'.
1. Oath-friend (Respect 75, Trust 50): She swears an oath on her own blade.

**Secrets** (light to deep).

| Layer | Title | How it opens | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Five she counts | Opens at Respect 20 | She walked seven orphans off the breaking ice but the sled carried twelve. She counts the five aloud at every new moon, and turns hard when anyone calls the story a legend. |
| 2 | The Moot's scout | Opens at Respect 40 | She is no exile. The Winter Moot made her an oath-holder and sent her south three winters ago to learn which villages would shelter the Frostbound if the winters never ease. She has told no one. |

## 12. Ilvane

*An Atlantean fleet-pilot who left the dome to guide the Covenant's convoys, and has begun to hear the sea go wrong.*

- **Id / portrait:** `ilvane` / `ilvane.webp`
- **Also called:** Ilvane the pilot, the Sea Folk pilot
- **People / region:** Sea Folk; Atlantis (lives at Abysswatch)
- **Calling:** Abyssal Gate pilot. **Soul Class** III. **Rank:** Fleet-pilot (contract).
- **Role:** Companion (pilot and navigator). The one person with a view of the sea and of the Witch's quiet worry; a far-away friend that the player earns by going to sea.

**Look.** Tall, about forty by her looks, with sea-pale skin faintly iridescent at the shoulders, long silver-black hair in a sailor's rope-braid and silver-grey eyes with a second lid that flickers in bright light. A thin clear sheath of membrane rests at her collar when she is dry. She carries a waterskin, brass pilot's dividers and a fish-bone chart-case, and moves carefully on dry decks.

**Voice.** Soft, exact and tidal: she pauses where a wave would come. Uses depths and distances for metaphors. Calls land-folk 'the Sheathless' with affection. Careful with surface idioms, and gets some wrong on purpose.

**Personality.** Calm, wry and watchful. Hates exaggeration and keeps her promises. Secretly lonely, and curious about loud music, hot food and the short, bright lives of humans. Distrusts temples. Happiest when useful.

**Background.** Born in Atlantis's Pearlquiet ward and trained as an Abyssal Gate pilot. For fourteen years she has worked the Tidebound Covenant's convoys out of Abysswatch as a pilot on contract, one of the few Sea Folk who live among the Sheathless. She keeps a tide-pool room above the Fleet Basin and never says why she left the dome.

**Where they start.** Abysswatch / Fleet Basin
- Goal: Keep the convoys running through the autumn gales.

## Lore gaps filled (please confirm or strike)

The lorebook and pack leave these open; the cast fills them. Everything else is lifted from the lore.

1. **The Ninth Brazier.** The lore gives a party of four with no names. Ruvai is the tracker; the party earned its A letter on a contract that killed a fifth member (a young healer); its leader holds the Grey Ledger mortgage. The other two members are left for the narrator to name.
2. **Bluewatch's lay sealer.** The lore says one exists and no priest will live there. She is Ugra, with a Cinder Host past.
3. **Ketil Ice-Oar's family.** Jorunn is Ketil's half-sister (same father, second hearth) and the Winter Moot's scout. The lore gives Ketil no kin and says nothing of Frostbound planning for the south.
4. **Somner's expedition.** The lore says it crossed the threshold and Somner is missing, and that the journal was found in his camp. Siebert is the lone survivor who left Somner alive at the camp, and Archmaster Quill has tried to buy his silence.
5. **Old Lantern Post, Hollin's Reach, Saint Orven's Ford, Stirrup Crossing.** The lore names the places but no one in them. The ford chapel, the infirmary corner and the Sealcrown cheap-hall are small invented rooms inside lore places.
6. **Highmark's spirit-binding trade.** The lore says binding is legal and a workshop craft in Highmark. The Lamp-Breaker rumour, the prefect's reward and the lantern ending in a noble's baggage in Prince Corvin's war party are new.
7. **Ironanvil teaching.** The lore says only a few elders read the Iron Archive's script. Bodvar teaching a pupil one page, and giving her a sealed letter, is new (and an ordinary secret, not a sealed truth).
8. **Inquest deserters.** The lore describes the Silent Cloisters and the hunt for otherworlders. A deserting Listener, an escaped captive and a cleric who reads the liturgy's Veil-script are new. The captive is left unnamed and her fate unknown.
9. **Atlantean pilots on land.** The lore says the Tidebound rely on Atlantean pilots and Atlantis forbids marriage with land peoples. Ilvane's exile, her sailor, the Archon's offer and the Witch's yearly letter asking for northern charts (before the public request in CY 613) are new. The Witch's dream stays untold: Ilvane does not know it.
10. **Broken Chain internals.** The lore says cells do not know each other and the Key-Widow may be only a title. Nib delivered the ribbon to a living bearer he will not name; the title's bearer is deliberately left unsaid.
11. **Goblin lifespans and ages.** The lore calls goblins short-lived without numbers; Nib at 24 and Ugra (orc) at 41 are set as old for their peoples.
12. **Meter presets.** The Ersveil pack defines no `meterPresets`. The entries use Story Suite's built-in Companion, Rival and Mentor presets and the pack's own stage shape. If CN1 expects `stages` as NSD ladder rungs (name plus a needs string) it is a one-line change.
13. **Cast limits.** C-COMMON gives no character limits except secrets (300) and event wording (120). The validator used: about 200, look 600, voice 400, personality 500, history 1200, goal 200, routine 300, each drawn-to phrase 60, each stage name 40 and line 200. These may need a trim when CS fixes the real caps.
14. **No elf in the twelve.** The Verdantleaf Enclaves keep their people home, and the lore's beastfolk heroes (Kaidu, Neshai) already exist, so there is one beastfolk (Ruvai) and no elf. If the USER wants up to four more (the cap is sixteen), good candidates are an elven Listener from Elenvar, a Wallwright engineer, a Border Kingdoms beacon-warden and a Ledgerford clerk or banker.

## Global style note for all portraits

Painterly semi-realistic fantasy portrait, muted grimdark palette with one warm light source, three-quarter or waist-up framing, shallow depth of field, a hint of the person's place behind them. No text, no logos, no gore. Adults only. Composed to read at a small square crop (the pack allows 64 KB per portrait) with the face in the upper third.
