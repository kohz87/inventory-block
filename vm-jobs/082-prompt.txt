Stage 5 (USER-approved design): let story-born goals become paid RPG quests, and let the story field monster variants, while staying provable. Today, in a rules-v2 chat, a quest offer must match a frozen pack template (else "no frozen quest template") and a foe must be a pack record. Ownership stays: Story Director owns threads (neutral goals: name, text, giver, place, state, deadline, Quests tab); RPG Battle owns the RPG extension (templates, objective proofs, rewards); Story Block owns proofs, claims, replay. Read RB src/v2/quests/ (capture, commands, settlement, credit, proofs, seams), src/v2/fight/foes.js, SB src/v2/packs/rules-v2.schema.json + rules-v2.js, SD threads, ~/story_suite/inventory-block/suite-notes/RB_PHASE0.md (quests, foes, §6 claims), RB_TRACK_B_DESIGN.md §B3, and 027-resume3.txt step 0 (the replay rule). Design principles: no guessing (explicit bindings only), nothing invents identity, code owns every number, deterministic on replay.

1. OPEN QUEST TEMPLATES.
   - Schema (SB rules-v2 schema + checker): a quest template may carry `open: true`. An open template's objectives may leave their target or text to the offer, within the template's allowed objective kinds (story/visit/kill/collect) and its requirements; its reward is a range, not a number: `reward: {coinMin, coinMax, xpMin, xpMax, items:[]}` for open templates only (closed templates unchanged). Bounds checked by the checker (within the pack's rank reward ranges).
   - Binding (explicit, never inferred): (a) the narrator's offer line `+ quest <Name> | like: <template slug> | text: <objective text> [| foe: <foe or variant>] [| place: <place>] [| item: <item>]`; (b) the reader's click "Make this a quest" on an existing SD thread with no RPG side (offers only open templates allowed here; goes through the refusal-reporting widget).
   - Opening check (RB, refused visibly on any failure, the thread stays a plain SD goal): requirements; objective kinds allowed by the template; targets valid (foe or variant allowed by the area, real place, real item); the reward is fixed by code from the template's range and the area tier (deterministic formula from the offer's evidence), recorded with the offer, never taken from the narrator.
   - Closing: unchanged machinery — objective proofs (sealed fight, inventory, scene, explicit met: yes), settlement once, retraction with causes, narrated-coin dedupe.
2. FOE VARIANTS.
   - A fight may name `<Variant name> | like: <pack foe slug>`: the variant copies the pack foe's skills, loot and tags and is scaled to the area's tier deterministically (level ≤ the area ceiling, stats within foeStatBudget, scaling from the recorded area and the base record only); its identity is derived (base foe + variant name), sealed into the fight result as a canonical ref (`<foe>~<variant-slug>`), and kill objectives match it by that ref or by its base when the template allows. Refused, never clamped, when it can't fit.
