## Follow-ups (none blocks)

1. Overlapping delays draw the party backwards (closest to a blocker; dates stay right). A second `~ trip delay` written while the first hold is still running double-counts the overlap in `positionOf`, so the km along, the "You" marker and the narrator's "N of M km" drop and stay low until the holds end. Repro (Ersveil, foot, Bluewatch to Wolfmere, set out Goldwane 12 8:00): `! time +2 days` (40/100 km); `~ trip delay: 1 day`; next reply `! time +12 hours` + `~ trip delay: 1 day`; next replies `! time +12 hours`: reads 40, 40, then 30 km and 30 km, expected 40 until the holds end. Arrival dates (Goldwane 22) are right. Unit repro: two holds at 2 d and 2.5 d of 1 d each on a 5-day leg make the along-km go 40, 40, 35, 30, 30, 35. A `~ trip` begun during the overlap would plan its mid-leg turn from the wrong km. Fix idea: start a hold at the later of its own time and the previous hold's end (or merge holds) in `tripOf`/`positionOf` (`src/v2/atlas/trip.js`). Two delays in one reply give the same effect. Add a test.
2. The trip counts no rest days and ignores a pack's route rules (board days, upstream rates); the Map's place-detail estimate (`travel.js`) adds one rest day per 6 active days. The two can disagree for the same journey (foot trip 8 days vs 9 in the estimate). The pace text says "riding, 25 km/day" for a trip whose river leg goes by boat at 35. Decide whether that is intended and say so in docs/dev/trips.md.
3. Line order inside one reply matters: `~ trip` before the `~ place` or `+ mount` lines it depends on gives "no place yet" or the default ride pace (a plain Problem or a quietly wrong pace, no damage). Worth one line in the grammar or docs.
4. After arrival the trip stays shown (and the tail keeps the cue "has reached X: write ~ place X") while the scene is at any place the trip named, including its starting point. Walking back to Bluewatch Village without `~ trip stop` keeps telling the narrator to move the party to Wolfmere Outpost. Fix idea: treat only the destination as "still arrived", or end the trip when the place changes from the destination.
5. Orphan delay records stay in `atlas.trips` after the set-out reply is deleted. Harmless (cleared by the next trip), but `tripsProblems` caps the map at 12 records, so a chat with eight delays on each of two trips whose first set-out is deleted could skip a later delay block on replay. Very rare.
6. Not from this release, seen while fuzzing: with Story Director and a pack installed, deleting the very first exchange (the reply that set the clock) when a later reply has `! time +8 hours` and `~ place` gives a replay-skip Problem "The weather has no text." Identical on main 2.10.0 + 2.4.0, so it needs its own ticket.
7. A delay written when the story clock is earlier than the trip's set-out is accepted but then ignored by `tripOf` (it sorts before the trip). Rare.
