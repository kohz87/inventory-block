## Blockers

### 5. Chronicle-gated local scenes are missed on a normal month step (B4 not fully closed)

The fix for B4 removed `after` and made the *date-based* scenes fire in the same generation as their world event (verified, see "B1-B4 status"). Eight scenes cannot do that, because their condition reads the chronicle entry that their world event writes, and the engine evaluates conditions against the view from the start of the generation. So the entry exists only on the next reply, and by then the reply's own time line has moved the clock:

- `marrowbridge-{corvin,hasse,wendhild}-scene` wait on "Highmark's war: X against Y" with a 20-day window (`minutes: 28800`).
- `crown-{wendhild,corvin,hasse}-scene` wait on "Reign of ..." with a 20-day window.
- `auvoth-evacuation-scene` and `last-wall-scene` have a 30-day window (`minutes: 43200`), which a 30-day step lands on exactly (`!passed(43200)` is false at +30d).

The clock moves at most 30 days per reply (I checked: `! time +45d`, `+2mo` and two stacked `+30d` lines all advance at most 30 days), so a plain "a month passes" reply right after the event closes all eight windows. The player's place never changed and the world event itself fired, but the scene and its hook are never delivered, which is exactly what review 169 B4 reported for the flood scene ("similar absolute-window `after` scenes deserve the same check"). With a 5-day step the same scene fires one reply later.

Repro (ran it; `repro-b5.mjs` is in the scratchpad, `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-inventory-block/3572f065-fdf3-5cf4-88eb-a15358a57ebe/scratchpad/rvscripts/repro-b5.mjs`; usage `SB_DIR=<Story_block> SD_DIR=<Story_director> node repro-b5.mjs 5d|30d`). It installs the pack, pins Court favour at the default 40/35/25, moves the player to Highmark Capital (`~ place Highmark Capital | kind: capital | region: Highmark Empire`), walks in 30-day steps to one hour before Haywane 20 CY 613, then sends the reply in which the battle fires with a clock step of 5d or 30d, and two more plain replies:

```
step 5d:  marrowbridge-hasse fired; marrowbridge-hasse-scene fired;   place region Highmark Empire; CY 613, Haywane 25, 09:00 am
step 30d: marrowbridge-hasse fired; marrowbridge-hasse-scene waiting; place region Highmark Empire; CY 613, Emberwane 20, 09:00 am
```

The same run pattern gave, with a 30d step and the player in `Highmark Empire`: `crown-wendhild-scene` waiting after `crown-wendhild-over-corvin` fired (CY 614, Greengale 1), `last-wall-scene` waiting after `last-wall-wendhild` fired (CY 615, Deepwinter 21), and with the player in `Auvoth Compact` `auvoth-evacuation-scene` waiting after `auvoth-falls` fired (CY 614, Deepwinter 25); each fires with a 5d step. A 40-reply walk with 30-day steps from Haywane 18 CY 613 with the player living in Highmark Empire also fired none of the three scenes.

Why it is a blocker and not only "no scene": the approved design gives the narrator the scene when the player is where it happens, and 169 B4 was accepted on exactly this. The narrator does still get the world event's cue (it is written for the narrator and says "Characters elsewhere do not know yet"), so nothing is corrupted; the local scene is what never fires.

Smallest fix I can see (not applied): give these eight scenes a window of more than 30 days (for example `minutes: 64800`, 45 days) so any single reply's clock step still lands inside it, or give each the parent's own time and branch test so it fires in the same generation (the battle scenes could read `time() >= 318023040` plus the same `least` test the sibling uses, kept exclusive by the war entry check). The first is one number per scene.
