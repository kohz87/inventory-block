## What changed and what remains

- Story Block rejects rules manifests and no longer supplies the battle rules capability. Its automatic per-reply story money setting is unlimited; an explicit value still applies the configured budget and associated item limits. Assist pre-roll and safety net, inventory, money, scene, data modules and the generic module SDK remain.
- Director uses standalone checks and story-told quests, and requires Story Block 2.6.0 or later. It no longer requires a battle provider or payment receipt to finish a counted story goal; quest types, rewards and difficulty remain descriptive text. The old battle quest schema, verified settlement code and completion controls were removed. Unsupported old quest data is retained opaquely with read-only guards; existing battle chats should stay on the earlier versions.
- Studio removes rules authoring and its unused class, skill-path and trainer editor assets. It retains world pack editing, calendar, weather, events and data modules. Studio and Director were refreshed by the documented Story Block sync scripts from `f74bab2e2e4dfcd6242a85ed86c615515c0fa3ea`.
- Ordinary pack fields for terms, ranks, skills, presets, crafts, markets and other optional content remain available as data and generic queries; the suite no longer uses the removed reader rank ladder. Some fields have no current in-suite consumer, so the lead should decide whether their continued availability is desired.
- NPC State Delta remains at `80a4532` and was used in the integration matrix.
