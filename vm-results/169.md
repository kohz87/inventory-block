## Blockers

### 1. Court favour starts with the wrong claimants at 35 and 25

`examples/packs/ersveil/pack.json:956` defines Wendhild **40**, Hasse **35**, Corvin **25**. The approved `ERSVEIL_PEOPLE.md` says Wendhild **40**, Corvin **35**, Hasse **25**. With Court favour enabled and no player actions, the code therefore breaks Corvin at Marrowbridge; applying the approved defaults to the same least-favour rule would break Hasse. This changes a normal, player-visible rules result. The new test encodes the reversed defaults as the expected result (`tests/v2/packs/ersveil-timeline.test.js:86`). The unpinned fallback still crowns Wendhild, as requested.

**Repro:** Enable Court favour in a new Ersveil chat and query `dataModules.view` for `pack.ersveil/succession`: it returns `{wendhild: 40, hasse: 35, corvin: 25}`. Advance normally to Haywane 20 CY 613 with no favour changes: `marrowbridge-corvin` fires. The real-host script below checks the values and the battle.

### 2. Succession variants are not exclusive after favour changes

The three battle events (`pack.json:866,872,878`) and six crown events (`:903-914`) each have their own once-only claim. They continue checking the *current* favour indefinitely and do not check whether a sibling has already decided that battle or crown. Once favour changes, another previously false branch can fire, and multiple battles can make multiple crown branches eligible. This is not a tie case: it occurs with ordinary, valid narrator `dm` lines.

**Repro, battle:** Enable the module; reach Haywane 20 CY 613 at defaults 40/35/25. `marrowbridge-corvin` fires. In two subsequent replies, have the player's deeds justify `- dm pack.ersveil/succession — undermine Hasse | kind: undermine | claimant: hasse | size: great`. Hasse falls 35 → 26 → 17. On the next generation `marrowbridge-hasse` also fires; the chronicle now records both Corvin killed and Hasse broken in the same battle.

**Independent repro, crown:** Start another enabled chat, leave favour unchanged, and advance to Rainmoot CY 614. Wendhild is crowned after Corvin's defeat. A single later valid `+ dm pack.ersveil/succession — support Hasse | kind: back | claimant: hasse | size: great` moves Hasse 35 → 44; next generation `crown-hasse-over-wendhild` fires as well. The chronicle contains both `Highmark crowns Empress Wendhild` and `Highmark crowns Emperor Hasse`. Both cases ran on the real host. The branch choice needs a durable shared decision for each milestone, not just one claim per variant.

### 3. Pruned resolved chronicle entries break later milestones and can repeat Auvoth's fall

All crown entries and `Auvoth is lost` are written as `state: resolved` with `keep: true` (`pack.json:903-914,932-933`). Story Director 2.3.0 retains at most 30 resolved chronicle entries, pruning the oldest even when locked or marked `keep`; `chronicle.passed` returns false when its entry has been pruned. But every Last Wall variant depends on a crown entry (`:941-943`), and the regular Auvoth fall uses the *absence* of `Auvoth is lost` as its exclusion guard (`:933`). A long ordinary campaign can accumulate resolved world events over the 19 months between crown and Last Wall.

**Repro, Last Wall never fires:** On the real host, use the default unpinned path and advance in 30-day steps until Wendhild is crowned in Rainmoot CY 614. Over the following 16 months, add 32 distinct, legitimate resolved `+ world ... | state: resolved` lines, one per reply, advancing 15 days per reply. The crown record is pruned at the normal 30-resolved cap. Advance past Frostfall 21 CY 615: all three `last-wall-*` variants remain waiting and `The Last Wall holds` is absent, so the post-Wall event also has no source. No manual deletion or modification of the pack is needed.

**Repro, Auvoth falls twice:** Enable Court favour, undermine Wendhild greatly four times and back Corvin greatly twice (4/43/35 for Wendhild/Corvin/Hasse). Advance to Nightfall CY 614 and witness `auvoth-falls-early`. Accumulate 32 additional distinct resolved world events with 15-day advances. Once the `Auvoth is lost` entry is pruned, the normal `auvoth-falls` becomes due and fires again, recreating the entry with a new `since`. In the real-host run, by Rainmoot CY 616 both fall event IDs were fired. The guards for irreversible branch decisions need an identity that survives normal chronicle pruning.

### 4. The local scene can expire after its parent fires while the player is present

A scene event waits for its parent claim, and the engine checks `after` against the view from the start of a generation. Thus the scene cannot fire in the generation in which the parent first fires. The scene also has an absolute deadline: the first flood scene requires time earlier than Haywane 16 (`pack.json:818-819`). A normal time advance in that same generated reply can pass the deadline. The player was in the specified region when the flood fired but never receives the scene and hook promised by the approved outline.

**Repro:** Start at the Bluewatch Guild Office; advance 30 days and two hours to Haywane 12, 08:40 CY 611. The early flood is due and the player is in `River Confederacy: Bluewatch`. Send a reply that shelters for five days (`! time +5d`). Before that reply, `flood-611` fires and writes `The early flood`; before the next reply it is Haywane 17, past the scene's cutoff. `flood-611-scene` remains waiting forever on the forward clock, even though the player's location never changed. The script below reproduces this on the real host. Similar absolute-window `after` scenes deserve the same check.

## Follow-ups

- `keep: true` on the resolved crown, Auvoth, and Last Wall entries does not put them in the narrator's every-reply tail: Director's `keptEvents` selects only looming/ongoing entries. This also makes the docs' promise that major changes are kept in mind too broad. The pruning consequence is blocker 3; the absent tail reminder is a separate follow-up.
- The approved outline dates the 611, 612, and 613 Turnings at Frostfall 21. The new decisive events start on Frostfall 25, 24, and 22 respectively (`pack.json:830,851,885`). The Ardelian outline says a new emperor is elected; the pack instead leaves the electors deadlocked under a regent (`:846`). These content differences do not independently meet the supplied blocker bar.
- Several news groups split an identical cue among independently claimed region variants to fit the condition length cap. The host correctly delays early-flood news six days, but after hearing `flood-611-news` in Highmark, entering Startrace can fire the identical `flood-611-news-2` bulletin again (`:820-821`). Other split groups have the same structure.
- All 56 new lore entries are nonconstant with whole-word matching. Multiword triggers still match ordinary phrases: for example `the Sparrow` matches a bird and `Quiet Hour` matches an ordinary quiet hour (`ersveil-lore.json` entries 215, 223). Some bare titles, such as `Guildmaster`, can also refer to someone else. These can insert unrelated biographies but do not disclose a future fate by themselves. Entry 180 is 301 characters against the roster's 200–300 target.
- `tests/v2/packs/ersveil-timeline.test.js` checks one static favour snapshot at a time, uses a stand-in chronicle without the Director cap, and walks in six-hour steps. It consequently misses changed favour, actual pruning, and a longer reply time jump. Add real-host coverage for these paths. `docs/dev/packs.md:33` also still calls the example Ersveil 2.0.0.
